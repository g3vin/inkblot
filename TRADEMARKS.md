# Trademark Policy

Inkblot's code is open source under the [Apache License 2.0](LICENSE). You can use,
change and share it, including commercially. The Inkblot name and logo aren't covered
by that licence. Section 6 of the licence says so, and this policy explains what you
can and can't do with them.

The goal is simple: when someone sees the Inkblot name or logo, they should be able
to trust that it is the app we make, or something that honestly says how it relates
to it.

## The marks

These are trademarks of Gavin Bowden (the "marks"):

- the name **Inkblot**, used for software, writing tools or related services;
- the **ib monogram** (`Mark` in `src/ui`);
- the **Inkblot wordmark** (`Wordmark` in `src/ui`);
- the **Inkblot app icon** (`AppIcon` in `src/ui`, and the files in `src-tauri/icons/`).

The source files for the logo (`src/ui/brandPaths.js`, `src/ui/Brand.jsx`,
`docs/brand/generator/` and the icon files) are in the repository under the Apache
licence, so you can read and build from them. Using the artwork as a logo is still
covered by this policy.

## What you can do without asking

- **Talk about Inkblot.** Use the name in articles, reviews, tutorials, videos,
  talks, courses and social posts.
- **Say what your work is for.** "A theme for Inkblot", "Converts Scrivener projects
  to Inkblot", "Compatible with Inkblot". Keep your own name first and don't use the
  logo as your logo.
- **Build and run it yourself.** Compile Inkblot from source for yourself or for
  people inside your organisation, unmodified, under its own name.
- **Share unmodified builds.** Redistribute an unmodified official release, or a build
  of an unmodified official release tag, as long as you don't imply that you are
  Inkblot or that we support your copy.
- **Link to us** with the name, or with the unmodified logo next to a link to the
  official site or repository.
- **Fork the code** for development and for pull requests back to this project.

## What needs a different name

If you distribute a **modified** version (a fork, a rebrand, a build with features
added or removed, or a build with different update or telemetry behaviour), then:

- Give it a name that isn't "Inkblot" and isn't confusingly similar. Names like
  "Inkblot Plus", "Inkblot CE" or "Inkblott" aren't allowed.
- Replace the monogram, wordmark and app icon with your own.
- Change the bundle identifier (`com.inkblot.app` in `src-tauri/tauri.conf.json`) and
  any update source, so your users' updates come from you.
- You may say, plainly, that it is "based on Inkblot" or "a fork of Inkblot".

## What isn't allowed

- Using the marks in a way that suggests we made, endorse or support something when
  we don't.
- Publishing an app, extension or listing in any store under the Inkblot name or with
  the Inkblot icon, unless it's our own release.
- Using the marks, or anything similar, in your product's name, company name, domain
  name or social handle.
- Changing, recolouring, redrawing or animating the logo, or combining it with other
  marks.
- Using the marks on merchandise you sell.
- Using the marks in anything misleading, unlawful or disparaging.

## Asking for permission

If you want to do something this policy doesn't allow, such as a user group, an
event, merchandise or a partnership, email **trademarks@inkblotwriting.studio**. We
are glad to hear from you, and most reasonable requests get a yes.

This policy may change as the project grows. The current version is the one in this
repository.
